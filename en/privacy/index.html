CopyHome Privacy Policy

CopyHome is a macOS app that backs up your servers to your Mac. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere: backups go straight from your servers to your Mac and to the storages you connected yourself.

Updated October 18, 2026

In short

  • CopyHome has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or ads.
  • CopyHome has no servers of its own. The developer never receives your backups, your passwords or any information about how you use the app.
  • Everything the app stores stays on your Mac.

What is stored on your Mac

  • Settings. Projects, server addresses, user names, databases, folders, schedules and connected storages. The settings file lives in ~/Library/Application Support/fast-backup and only your macOS account can read it.
  • Passwords and keys. Database and SSH passwords, S3 access keys, Yandex Disk and Google Drive tokens and the backup encryption password. They are encrypted with a key from the macOS Keychain and never stored in plain text.
  • Backups and history. Backup files go to the folder you chose; the run log and your servers' key fingerprints are kept in the app's folder. Restores and trial restores also happen only on this Mac: in a local database, in Docker or in a folder.
  • The file for moving to a new Mac. It exists only when you save your settings yourself. If you include passwords, it is encrypted with your password in the age format. Yandex Disk and Google Drive tokens never go into it.

What the app connects to

  • Your servers over SSH. CopyHome dumps databases and archives folders and streams them straight into a file on your Mac. No copies are left on the server.
  • The storages you connected. Yandex Disk, Google Drive, S3-compatible services (Yandex Object Storage, Selectel, VK Cloud, Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2, AWS or your own server), WebDAV servers (Nextcloud, ownCloud, Synology, Koofr and others) and folders on your Mac, an external drive or a NAS. Only your backups go there, plus a small file listing them with their checksums. Those services' own terms govern how they store your data.
  • Yandex and Google sign-in pages. When you connect a drive, sign-in opens in your browser. CopyHome only receives an access token and never sees your password.
  • GitHub, for automatic updates. CopyHome checks for new versions at github.com/ilyabazhenov/copyhome and downloads them from there. As with any request, GitHub sees your Mac's IP address. The app sends no information about you or your backups. You can turn automatic updates off in Settings; CopyHome then contacts GitHub only when you choose “Check for Updates…”.

Yandex Disk

CopyHome asks only for access to its app folder “Applications/CopyHome” and to the Disk's size, to show free space. The rest of your Disk is not available to the app. If a storage was connected in an early version with access to the whole Disk, CopyHome offers to connect it again. You can revoke access in your Yandex ID settings.

Google Drive

CopyHome requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. With it the app sees only the files and folders it created itself; the rest of your Drive is not available to it. It also reads the account's email address and storage usage to show them in the storage settings.

  • Data from Google Drive is used only to upload your backups, list them, download them back and delete old ones according to your retention settings.
  • It is not shared with third parties, not sold, and not used for advertising or to train artificial intelligence models. The data is processed only on your Mac, so no person reads it.
  • The access token is stored encrypted on your Mac. You can disconnect Google Drive by removing the storage in CopyHome, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

CopyHome's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The copyhome.app website

The website has no cookies, analytics or counters. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, fonts load from Google Fonts and the app downloads from GitHub, so those services see your IP address and browser, as with any request. Your theme choice is saved only in your browser.

Deleting your data

Remove your storages and projects in CopyHome, then delete the app and the ~/Library/Application Support/fast-backup folder. Backups on your Mac and in your storages remain your own files; delete them as usual.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
